About Shafi Chowdhury

Approximately 33 years across pharmaceutical operations, clinical research and clinical statistical programming, and Founder of a specialist clinical programming company since 2001.

Biography

Pharmaceutical industry professional, entrepreneur and Managing Director

Shafi Chowdhury has spent approximately 33 years in the pharmaceutical industry, in roles spanning pharmaceutical operations, clinical research and clinical statistical programming. His professional background lies in statistical programming for clinical trials: the work that turns clinical data into the analyses and reports on which development decisions depend.

In 2001 he founded Shafi Consultancy Ltd., a specialist clinical programming company that works with pharmaceutical companies and contract research organisations. As Founder and Managing Director, he has combined technical understanding with the practical demands of running a business, from building teams to maintaining international client relationships.

Throughout his career, Shafi has taken a particular interest in quality, in programming innovation and in developing the people who do this work.

Today he leads Shafi Consultancy Ltd. as Founder and Managing Director, responsible for the company's direction, its client relationships and the development of its people.
