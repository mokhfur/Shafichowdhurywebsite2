Leadership & entrepreneurship

Building expertise, developing people, delivering quality

In 2001 Shafi Chowdhury founded Shafi Consultancy Ltd., a specialist clinical programming company. He has led it as Managing Director since.

Shafi Consultancy and its development

The story of the company

Visit Shafi Consultancy
  1. 2001

    Founding a specialist company

    Shafi founded Shafi Consultancy Ltd. in 2001 to provide clinical statistical programming as a focused, specialist service rather than one function among many.

  2. Capability

    Developing statistical programming capability

    The company's work centres on statistical programming for clinical trials, including standards-driven data and clinical pharmacology deliverables. Building that capability has meant investing in skills, processes and tools.

  3. Clients

    Working with pharmaceutical companies and CROs

    Shafi has built working relationships with international pharmaceutical companies and contract research organisations, providing programming support that fits into their own development programmes.

  4. People

    Developing and mentoring professionals

    He has supported the training, mentoring and career development of the programmers and staff who deliver the company's work.

  5. Culture

    Integrity, commitment and teamwork

    He has encouraged a culture centred on integrity, commitment and teamwork, with quality, innovation and operational efficiency as everyday expectations.

Business philosophy

A specialist business is built on the quality of its work, the development of its people and the trust of its clients.

Quality

Clinical outputs are relied on by statisticians, clinicians and regulators. Accuracy and dependable process sit at the centre of the work.

People

A specialist service depends on specialist people. Training, mentoring and career development are treated as part of the business, not an addition to it.

Long-term relationships

Working relationships with pharmaceutical companies and CROs are built over years, through consistency and integrity.

Leadership and management

The role of Managing Director

  1. Direction

    Setting the company's direction and the standards for the services it provides to pharmaceutical companies and CROs.

  2. Operations

    Building and managing specialist clinical programming operations so that work is delivered consistently and to schedule.

  3. Development

    Developing statistical programming capability and supporting the careers of the people who deliver it.
